Hearing the world

A chime and a short buzz when the world has a moment — a betrayal, a wall standing, a confession. Off by default; nothing is downloaded, the tones are made on the device.

The world away from this device

Left empty, this app keeps its own world, and time missed while it was closed is paid back on the next launch. Point it at a world that is already running somewhere else (the README's "always-on world") and the world ticks whether or not this app is open: what happened while you were gone is waiting in the Chronicle when you come back.

World save insurance

Download the complete simulated world — minds, memories, relationships, secrets, project progress, and replay state — or restore it later from a backup JSON file. Import replaces the current local world; API keys, chats, and keeper progress are not included. Stop the background service before restoring its world.
